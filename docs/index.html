Frequently asked questions

How is the price calculated?

The estimate depends on service type, size, bedrooms, bathrooms, frequency, and add-ons. The final price is confirmed before cleaning starts.

How far in advance should I book?

You can request a preferred date and time in the booking form. Same-day or next-day availability depends on schedule and service area.

Do you bring cleaning supplies?

Yes, standard supplies and equipment can be included. Add a note if you prefer specific products.

How long does cleaning take?

A standard home cleaning usually takes about 2 hours. Deep, move-out, office, and post-construction cleanings take longer depending on size and condition.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. Send a reschedule request as early as possible so the team can confirm a new time.
